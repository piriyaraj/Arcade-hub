🎮 Game Controls & Rules

Movement: WASD or Arrow Keys to pilot the Defense Ship.

Aim & Fire: Mouse pointer to aim, Left Click to fire Beacon Plasma Beams.

Surge Shield: Hold E or Right Click to activate Kinetic Surge Shield (drains energy).

EMP Nova: Press Q or Space to detonate EMP Beacon Pulse Nova (clears enemies & projectiles).

Objective: Protect the Central Beacon Core and eliminate incoming virus drones and Beacon Dreadnought Bosses.
